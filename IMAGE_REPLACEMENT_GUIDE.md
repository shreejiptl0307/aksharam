## 💡 Best Practices for Replacing Images

1. **Format Recommendation**: Save your final assets as high-quality WebP or compressed JPG (quality ~82–85%).
2. **File Naming**: Overwrite the existing files with the exact same filenames (e.g. `hero-main.jpg`, `collection-lehenga.jpg`), or update the image references in `src/data/collections.ts`.
3. **Color Profile**: Use sRGB color space for consistent web rendering across Apple displays, OLED screens, and mobile devices.
4. **Artistic Consistency**: Ensure model photography maintains warm neutral / muted editorial tones to harmonize with Aksharam's Warm Ivory (`#F7F3EC`), Charcoal (`#181715`), and Burgundy (`#702C36`) brand identity.
